INTRODUCTION
Rationale of the Study
Motorcycle maintenance is important in keeping motorcycles safe, reliable, and suitable for everyday use. As motorcycle use continues to be part of daily transportation, proper servicing is needed to prevent maintenance problems and unexpected breakdowns. However, motorcycle service operations may still involve manual scheduling, long waiting times, and difficulties in monitoring maintenance records. A study by Bondad et al. (2023) developed MotorCycleApp to help motorcycle owners find available mechanics and send service requests, showing how digital technology can improve access to motorcycle repair and maintenance services. Similarly, Farizqi et al. (2024) developed an Android-based motorcycle service booking application that allowed customers to select their preferred service schedule and helped reduce the need to wait at the workshop. These studies show the usefulness of digital systems in improving motorcycle service transactions. The proposed study is also related to Sustainable Development Goal 9 (SDG 9): Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure, particularly in the use of technology and innovation to improve industry and service operations. In the proposed system, customers will be able to use the web-based platform to manage their motorcycle service needs without relying entirely on manual transactions. The customer can access the system, view the motorcycle's maintenance and warranty information, select the needed service, and choose an available date, time slot, and mechanic. The system will also provide maintenance and warranty reminders through SMS to help customers remember their scheduled services. On the dealership side, staff can manage customer and motorcycle records, service schedules, maintenance information, and other after-sales service activities. After a service is completed, the maintenance record can be updated so that the customer can view the motorcycle's service history and upcoming maintenance needs. This workflow is related to the approach used by Farizqi et al. (2024), where customers could select their preferred service schedule through a digital booking application and service providers could manage the submitted bookings.
 Mindanao Eversure Corporation, specifically its motorcycle after-sales service operations, does not have an automated Motorcycle Service Scheduling and Monitoring System to ensure the efficiency and accuracy of managing motorcycle maintenance and customer after-sales services. These are the following problems that Mindanao Eversure Corporation is currently experiencing: (1) the absence of an automated maintenance reminder system can cause customers to miss their scheduled preventive maintenance and warranty service dates, resulting in delayed servicing that may affect motorcycle performance and warranty validity; (2) customers who experience sudden motorcycle breakdowns have no immediate and formal channel to alert the dealership, as existing phone and walk-in based processes are often delayed, unanswered outside business hours, and difficult to coordinate when the customer is stranded, resulting in prolonged response times, increased safety risk, and reduced customer trust in the dealership's after-sales service; (3) the absence of an organized service scheduling and selection system can make it difficult for customers to conveniently choose service types, available dates, time slots, and mechanics, resulting in manual booking processes, scheduling conflicts, longer waiting times, and inefficient service coordination; (4) customers have a lack of ways to understand the motorcycle's overall condition, as maintenance history, mileage, inspection results, and overdue services are not consolidated into a single indicator, forcing reliance on staff's verbal assessment and leading to overlooked maintenance needs, delayed repairs, and increased risk of unexpected breakdowns; and lastly, (5) the manual preparation of maintenance reports and service records can cause delays and inaccuracies in monitoring maintenance activities, customer return rates, and dealership performance, resulting in slower decision-making.
To address these concerns, the researchers propose the development of the Motorcycle Service Scheduling and Monitoring System for Mindanao Eversure Corporation. The proposed web-based system is designed to provide automated SMS reminders for preventive maintenance and warranty schedules, organized service scheduling and selection, motorcycle maintenance monitoring, warranty management, customer service history, and maintenance reports and analytics. Through these functions, the system aims to help customers keep track of their motorcycle maintenance while allowing dealership staff to manage service activities and records in a more organized manner. The proposed system will integrate these services into one platform instead of handling them through separate manual processes. In this way, the system is expected to help reduce missed maintenance schedules, improve service coordination, minimize errors in record management, and support more efficient after-sales service operations at Mindanao Eversure Corporation. Automated SMS Maintenance and Warranty Notification
The system aims to remind customers about their upcoming preventive maintenance, required service intervals, and warranty expiration dates to reduce missed service schedules and encourage timely motorcycle servicing. It monitors the maintenance and warranty information of each registered motorcycle and automatically sends SMS notifications to the corresponding customer when a scheduled maintenance service or warranty expiration date is approaching, helping customers stay informed and maintain their motorcycles regularly.
 Emergency Service Request
The system aims to provide customers with a formal and accessible channel for requesting immediate assistance when their motorcycle experiences an unexpected breakdown or critical mechanical problem. It allows customers to submit an emergency service request by providing details about the motorcycle's problem and, optionally, their current location. Dealership staff can then view, manage, and respond to the submitted requests, allowing the dealership to coordinate assistance more efficiently and reduce delays in responding to customers experiencing motorcycle emergencies.
Service Scheduling and package customization
The system aims to provide customers with a convenient and organized method for selecting motorcycle services and scheduling appointments while reducing manual booking, scheduling conflicts, and customer waiting time. It allows customers to select their preferred service, available date, time slot, and mechanic based on the dealership's current service availability. Before confirming an appointment, the system checks the availability of the selected service and mechanic to prevent overlapping schedules and ensure that the appointment can be properly accommodated by the dealership.  Motorcycle Health Score
The system aims to help customers understand the overall maintenance condition of their motorcycle and identify potential maintenance needs. It evaluates relevant motorcycle information, including maintenance history, mileage, inspection results, and overdue services, and uses these factors to calculate an overall health score.  Maintenance Analytics and Reporting

It collects and processes service and maintenance records and automatically generates reports, summaries, and analytical information that can be reviewed by management. These reports help management identify service trends, evaluate dealership operations, monitor customer return rates, and support faster and more informed decision-making.
Limitation of the Study
The study is limited to the development of a web-based Motorcycle Service Scheduling and Monitoring System for Mindanao Eversure Corporation in Tupi, South Cotabato, focusing on preventive maintenance reminders, emergency service requests, service scheduling and selection, motorcycle health assessment, and maintenance analytics and reporting. The system is intended only for motorcycles serviced by the dealership and does not cover other types of vehicles or services outside the dealership's after-sales operations. The system does not perform actual motorcycle diagnosis, repair, or physical inspection, as these activities remain the responsibility of qualified dealership personnel. In addition, emergency service requests depend on the availability and response of dealership staff, while SMS notifications depend on the availability of the integrated SMS service and mobile network connectivity. The study does not include online payment processing, spare-parts inventory management, or real-time motorcycle tracking. Add all limitation

Definition of Terms 
Automated SMS Notification – A system function that automatically sends text messages to customers regarding upcoming motorcycle maintenance schedules and warranty expiration dates.
Customer Portal – An online section of the system where customers can access their motorcycle information, maintenance records, schedules, and warranty status.
Emergency Service Request – A system function that allows customers to report unexpected motorcycle breakdowns or critical mechanical problems and request assistance from the dealership.
Maintenance Analytics – The process of analyzing collected maintenance and service data to identify trends, customer visits, frequently performed services, and dealership performance.
Maintenance History – A record of previous maintenance services, inspections, repairs, and other service activities performed on a motorcycle.
Maintenance Schedule – The planned dates or intervals when a motorcycle should undergo preventive maintenance or required servicing.
Motorcycle Health Score – A calculated indicator that represents the overall maintenance condition of a motorcycle based on factors such as mileage, maintenance history, inspection results, and overdue services.
Preventive Maintenance – Regular motorcycle servicing performed at scheduled intervals to maintain performance and help prevent unexpected mechanical problems.
Service Scheduling – The process of selecting and organizing a motorcycle service appointment based on the available service, date, time slot, and mechanic.
Warranty Monitoring – The process of tracking the warranty status and expiration date of a customer's motorcycle to help ensure that warranty-related services are not missed.

Motorcycle Service Scheduling and Digital Booking
Recent foreign literature highlights the use of digital technologies to improve motorcycle service scheduling and customer transactions. Farizqi et al. (2024) developed an Android-based motorcycle service booking application that allows customers to schedule maintenance appointments electronically, reducing dependence on manual booking procedures. Wasis et al. (2024) similarly developed a web- and Android-based motorcycle service and maintenance application that provides online service booking and maintenance-related information. These studies demonstrate that digital scheduling can improve customer convenience, organize service appointments, and reduce scheduling difficulties. However, the systems primarily focus on booking and maintenance management and do not provide a complete dealership-based solution that also handles emergency requests, motorcycle condition assessment, automated SMS reminders, and maintenance analytics.
Preventive Maintenance and Service Monitoring
Preventive maintenance management has also been supported by computerized systems. Barreiro-Zambrano et al. (2026) implemented an integrated preventive maintenance management system that automatically generates maintenance schedules and service alerts for light vehicles. Similarly, Qamarina et al. (2026) developed a mobile application for vehicle maintenance and scheduling that allows users to monitor maintenance intervals, organize service records, and receive maintenance notifications. These studies show that automated monitoring and notifications can encourage timely maintenance and reduce missed service activities. However, they do not combine preventive maintenance reminders with motorcycle service scheduling, emergency service requests, motorcycle health assessment, and dealership-level maintenance reporting in one system.
Vehicle Service Operations and Customer Assistance
Digital systems have also been used to improve service operations and customer assistance. Sugianto et al. (2026) examined technician performance and customer waiting time in motorcycle service workshops, emphasizing the importance of efficient service operations in improving service quality. Surende Babu and Ms. N. D. (2026) proposed a Vehicle Services System that integrates service requests, maintenance management, and customer information into a centralized platform. These studies indicate that organized service requests and centralized information can improve operational efficiency and customer service. However, the reviewed systems do not specifically provide an emergency service request process combined with automated maintenance and warranty SMS notifications, service scheduling and mechanic selection, motorcycle health scoring, and maintenance analytics.
Local Literature
Philippine Motorcycle and Vehicle Maintenance Context
Philippine literature also demonstrates the application of digital technologies to motorcycle and vehicle maintenance. Bondad et al. (2023) developed MotorCycleApp, a mobile application that helps motorcycle owners locate nearby mechanics and repair shops, improving access to maintenance services. Amores (2021) developed a Vehicle Maintenance and Monitoring System with a GPS tracking device that supports maintenance schedule monitoring and vehicle location tracking. Flores et al. (2023) developed a Vehicle Fixing Recommender System using user-based collaborative filtering to recommend appropriate repair services, while Sobejana (2021) developed an automated lead-acid battery detector and monitoring system for vehicles. These studies demonstrate the growing use of technology for motorcycle and vehicle maintenance, monitoring, and service assistance. However, the reviewed systems address individual maintenance concerns rather than providing an integrated dealership-based platform for service scheduling, emergency requests, automated SMS notifications, motorcycle health assessment, and maintenance reporting.
Local Service Quality and Motorcycle Operations
The importance of organized motorcycle service operations is further supported by local research on service quality and workplace practices. Sarita and Bautista (2025) examined occupational safety practices among motorcycle repair shops and emphasized the importance of proper procedures, organized repair operations, and effective management in maintaining service quality and workplace efficiency. Balinado et al. (2021) also found that reliable, responsive, and efficient automotive after-sales service contributes to customer satisfaction and loyalty. These findings emphasize the importance of improving service processes and customer responsiveness within motorcycle and automotive service establishments. However, these studies do not provide a system that automates customer maintenance reminders, emergency service requests, appointment scheduling, motorcycle condition assessment, and maintenance analytics.
Relevance to Mindanao Eversure Corporation
In the context of Mindanao Eversure Corporation in Tupi, South Cotabato, the identified problems are related to the dealership's after-sales service processes. Customers may miss preventive maintenance and warranty schedules when reminders are not automated, while customers experiencing motorcycle breakdowns may have difficulty immediately notifying the dealership through existing phone or walk-in procedures. Manual service booking can also result in scheduling conflicts, limited choices of service dates and mechanics, and longer waiting times. In addition, motorcycle maintenance information such as mileage, maintenance history, inspection results, and overdue services may not be consolidated into an understandable indicator, making it difficult for customers to recognize their motorcycle's maintenance condition. Manual preparation of maintenance records and reports may also delay management's ability to monitor service activities and dealership performance.
Research Gaps and Limitations
The reviewed literature shows that existing systems provide useful solutions for individual motorcycle and vehicle service concerns, particularly digital booking, preventive maintenance scheduling, service monitoring, and customer assistance. However, these solutions remain fragmented and do not provide a single web-based system specifically designed for the after-sales service operations of a motorcycle dealership. The proposed system addresses these gaps through five integrated features: Automated SMS Maintenance and Warranty Notification to reduce missed maintenance and warranty schedules; Emergency Service Request to provide customers with a formal channel for reporting motorcycle breakdowns and requesting assistance; Service Scheduling and Selection to organize appointments based on service, date, time slot, and mechanic availability; Motorcycle Health Score to provide an understandable assessment based on mileage, maintenance history, inspection results, and overdue services; and Maintenance Analytics and Reporting to provide management with organized information for monitoring maintenance activities, customer visits, service trends, and dealership performance. Therefore, the proposed system provides a more comprehensive approach to motorcycle after-sales service management by integrating these functions into one centralized web-based platform for Mindanao Eversure Corporation, Tupi, South Cotabato.
CHAPTER III
RESEARCH METHODOLOGY AND DESIGN
This chapter presents the methodology and design used by the researchers in developing the proposed system. It describes the research environment, software development methodology, system development phases, and design tools used throughout the development process. It also covers the system’s functional requirements, database design, network design, technology stack, and project development activities.
Environment
The study will be conducted at Mindanao Eversure Corporation, Tupi, South Cotabato, Philippines. The organization serves as the study environment where the researchers will identify the existing processes related to motorcycle maintenance, service scheduling, customer service, and maintenance monitoring. The environment provides the necessary setting for determining the requirements and developing a web-based system suited to the needs of the dealership, its customers, dealership staff, and management.
Software Development Methodology
The Rapid Application Development (RAD) Model will be used as the software development methodology for the proposed system because it emphasizes rapid prototyping, continuous user feedback, and iterative improvement. It consists of four phases: Requirements Planning, where the researchers identify the dealership’s problems and system requirements; User Design, where the system interface, workflow, and database are designed and refined based on feedback; Construction, where the proposed system is programmed, integrated, and tested; and Cutover, where the completed system undergoes final testing, user evaluation, corrections, and deployment. This methodology is relevant because it allows the researchers to continuously improve the system while developing its service scheduling, maintenance monitoring, emergency service request, motorcycle condition monitoring, automated notification, and reporting functions.

Requirements Planning
In this phase, the researchers identify and analyze the existing processes, problems, and requirements of Mindanao Eversure Corporation related to motorcycle maintenance and after-sales services. The researchers gather information about customer service, maintenance schedules, service appointments, emergency requests, motorcycle records, and reporting procedures. The output of this phase is a set of documented system requirements that will serve as the basis for designing the proposed system.

In this phase, the researchers develop the initial system design based on the identified requirements. This includes designing the user interface, system workflows, database structure, and system functions, followed by reviewing and refining the designs based on feedback. Construction
In this phase, the researchers transform the approved designs and requirements into a functional web-based system. Cutover
In this phase, the completed system undergoes final testing and evaluation to ensure that its functions operate according to the identified requirements. Necessary corrections and improvements are applied before the system is prepared for implementation and use by the intended users. The output is a finalized and functional system ready for deployment at Mindanao Eversure Corporation.

Operation Concepts
The Motorcycle Service Scheduling and Monitoring System automates the management of motorcycle service data, the processing of service appointments, tracking of motorcycle health, filtering eligible service schedules, and generating reports. Staff manage customer and motorcycle records, update statuses, and track service completion. The system ensures security through role-based access control, maintaining integrity with regular backups and updates. By automating these processes, the system improves efficiency and reduces errors in managing motorcycle after-sales service data.

Network Design 
The proposed Motorcycle Service Scheduling and Monitoring System for Mindanao Eversure Corporation will use a Client-Server Network with a Star Topology. The dealership will operate a Local Area Network (LAN) for staff, while customers connect through the Wide Area Network (WAN)/Internet. The web and database servers sit at the center of the star, and all client devices connect through a central router, firewall, and switch.
USE CISCO PACKET
Network Topology
The proposed system will use a Star Topology with a Client-Server architecture. The central device is the Core Switch / Router, which connects all internal network components. The Firewall and Router act as the gateway to the Internet, allowing customers and staff to access the system.
